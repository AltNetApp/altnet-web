Supported Games

EVE Online

AltNet supports connecting EVE Online characters through EVE’s official authorization system and uses supported EVE developer interfaces to provide character and game-related features.

AltNet does not ask for or receive your EVE Online account password. Authorization is handled through the official EVE sign-in flow, and the permissions available to AltNet depend on the access you approve and the current EVE developer interfaces.

AltNet may display or process EVE-related information made available through official developer interfaces, authorized character access, or legitimate public game data. Availability and accuracy of that information may depend on the systems operated by the applicable EVE Game Provider.

AltNet does not block EVE Online game data, EVE-related content, or EVE-related AltNet functionality behind a real-money paywall or premium subscription. AltNet Supporter is an optional subscription that helps cover AltNet’s operating costs. Its current benefit is removal of supported feed advertising. Purchasing AltNet Supporter is not required to view or use EVE-related content or functionality available through AltNet.
